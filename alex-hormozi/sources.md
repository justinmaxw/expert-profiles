# Sources — `alex-hormozi`

Evidence file for the Alex Hormozi method profile. Every non-obvious claim in
`SKILL.md` should trace back to a row below.

**Methodology note (read this first):** this research pass was done via live
web search and page/transcript fetches — not by watching full video or
listening to full audio. Where a claim comes from a podcast or YouTube video,
the "Accessed via" column says whether I saw a transcript, a summary site
(e.g. Shortform's episode summaries), or the platform page itself, as
distinct from the original audio/video. That is a real limit on
verifiability: transcript and summary sites can compress, paraphrase, or
misdate an episode. Treat quotes marked "via summary" as close paraphrase
unless independently checked against the original. Nothing below is a quote
I did not see the text of; nothing was invented.

## Known gaps

- I could not independently fetch a single primary announcement page for the
  Skool equity stake (e.g. a specific dated post from Hormozi announcing it);
  the date and framing in `SKILL.md` §7 rests on multiple independent
  secondary reports agreeing, not a primary source I read directly. Flagged
  as lower-confidence and dated approximately ("late 2023 / January 2024").
- The Diary of a CEO transcript's original recording/air date could not be
  confirmed independently of the transcript-hosting site's own page date —
  quotes from it are used because their content is highly specific and
  plausible in his voice, but the exact date attached to them should be
  treated as approximate.
- I did not watch or listen to any source directly end-to-end; all podcast
  and video material was accessed through transcripts, platform listings,
  or third-party episode-summary sites. This is a meaningful limit — a
  future revision of this profile should verify high-stakes quotes (the
  ones in quotation marks in `SKILL.md` §5 and §7) against original audio
  where possible.
- Net worth and precise portfolio revenue figures found during research
  varied across sources (from ~$100M to ~$350M net worth claims) and were
  deliberately left out of `SKILL.md` — they're not load-bearing for the
  method, and the range was too inconsistent across sources to state a
  single number confidently.
