# Frameworks cheat-sheet — Alex Hormozi

Quick-reference table version of `SKILL.md` §4. Use `SKILL.md` for the full
usage rules and limits — this is a scan-and-recall aid, not a substitute.

| Framework | Formula / structure | Fires when |
|---|---|---|
| Value Equation | (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) ÷ (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice) | Offer isn't converting, or deciding what to add before what to charge |
| Grand Slam Offer | Dream outcome → problem decomposition → bonuses (reserve / close / delight) → guarantee → scarcity/urgency → name & price | Product is fine but conversion/pricing power is weak |
| Guarantees | Unconditional / Conditional / Performance-implied / Anti-guarantee | Match type to how much of the outcome you actually control |
| Pricing | Price on value, not cost or competitors; beta-price low, raise until real resistance | Setting or revisiting price; never as the first lever on a delivery problem |
| Core Four | Warm outreach / Cold outreach / Content / Paid ads | Confirmed lead-constrained (not offer-constrained) business |
| Rule of 100 | 100 units/day of one Core Four action, sustained | Setting an execution floor once the right channel is chosen |
| Money Models | Attraction → Upsell → Downsell → Continuity; phased Get Cash → Get More Cash → Get Most Cash | Offer is already validated; question is monetization sequencing |
| Money-model targets | LTV:CAC ≥ 5:1, 30-day cash multiplier ≥ 2x, churn < 3%/mo | Evaluating whether a money model is scalable |
| Theory of constraints | Business grows to its constraint, no further | Always step 2 of the attack sequence — find the bottleneck before prescribing |
| Supply vs. demand diagnostic | "Double ad spend tomorrow — chaos (supply) or 2x sales (demand)?" | Classifying the constraint before choosing a fix |
| Leads vs. offer vs. sales diagnostic | Low close rate everywhere = offer problem; good economics + low volume = leads problem | Response to "I need more leads" |
| Hook / Retain / Reward | Hook (stop scroll) → Retain (deliver value) → Reward (specific takeaway) | Building a content engine |
| Give-away-for-free | Full method free → self-selects execution-minded, scaling founders → deal-flow/inbound funnel | Deciding whether to publish the "whole" method vs. teasers |
| Hiring bar | "Rhino, horse, and fireflies," not a unicorn; minimal rules = personnel problem | Hiring decisions and reading team-standard problems |
| Let fires burn | Don't personally solve every flare-up; protect time for the actual constraint | Operator is stuck in firefighting instead of on the bottleneck |
