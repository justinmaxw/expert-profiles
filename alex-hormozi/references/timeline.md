# Timeline — Alex Hormozi (operating history)

Dated milestones as they shape business decisions and the evolution of his
public method, not a fan-wiki life story. See `../sources.md` for citations.
Some dates below are approximate where sources only converge on a range —
marked "~".

- **2013** — Opens first gym, United Fitness, in Huntington Beach, CA,
  funded with ~$60k in personal savings.
- **2013-2014** — Sleeps on the gym floor during the early build-out period
  (confirmed in his own words on the Young and Profiting podcast); expands
  to six locations within three years, but the business becomes
  over-leveraged with debt — the near-bankruptcy period he references
  repeatedly as formative.
- **2016** — Alex and Leila Hormozi found Gym Launch, selling systems
  (offer, sales, and operations improvements) to other gym owners. Reaches
  $17M in revenue by year three via cold outreach, without ad spend — the
  origin of the warm/cold-outreach-first instinct documented in §4/§6 of
  `SKILL.md`.
- **2019** — Launches Prestige Labs, a supplement company built as a
  profit-center add-on for gyms.
- **2020** — Founds ALAN, a software company for brick-and-mortar customer
  acquisition.
- **2021** — Gym Launch and Prestige Labs sold to private equity for
  $46.2M; ALAN sold separately in an all-stock deal. Publishes **$100M
  Offers**. This exit marks the shift from single-vertical (gym/fitness)
  operator to Acquisition.com-era portfolio thinking — see `SKILL.md` §7.
- **2021-present** — Builds Acquisition.com with Leila Hormozi as a
  portfolio/holding company: invests in already-profitable, founder-owned,
  cash-flow-positive businesses doing $3M+ revenue, adding systems and
  distribution rather than just capital.
- **2023** — Publishes **$100M Leads**.
- **~late 2023 / announced ~January 2024** — Acquires an equity stake in
  Skool (the community platform founded by Sam Ovens in 2019), publicly
  described as one of his largest personal investments; reported
  correlated with a large jump in Skool's traffic and user base. Marks a
  move from pure content/education business toward platform/infrastructure
  ownership — flagged in `SKILL.md` §7 as directional evidence, not a
  universal prescription.
- **2025-08-16** — Publishes **$100M Money Models**, widening the public
  method from offer design and lead generation into full monetization
  sequencing (attraction/upsell/downsell/continuity, CAC:LTV, cash-flow
  timing).
- **2025-2026** — Recent podcast and interview material (see `sources.md`
  for specific dated episodes) shows continued emphasis on constraint
  diagnosis ("supply vs. demand," "why 'not scalable' is the wrong
  diagnosis") and a more cautious, tool-not-oracle stance on AI in business
  decision-making than the broader "AI changes everything" discourse of the
  period.
